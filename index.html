How the Adaptive (CAT) Engine Works

  • One question at a time. Answer it, and you immediately see whether you were right or wrong — followed by an explanation of the correct option and of why every other option does not apply.
  • Every answer updates your ability estimate (a Rasch/3PL-style θ with a Bayesian posterior). The engine then selects the next question at the difficulty that measures you most precisely — easier items after a miss, harder items after a hit.
  • The exam can end early. Once the estimate is precise enough (standard error ≤ 0.36) and at least 25 items have been answered, the engine stops — exactly how the real CISSP CAT terminates. Maximum length is all 125 questions in the bank.
  • Scoring: raw score, percentage, and a CISSP-style scaled score of 100–1000 where 700 is the pass standard. Content is balanced across the eight CISSP domains.
  • One item is “select all that apply” with five options and several correct answers — tick every correct option before submitting it. It is graded like a CISSP-style item: all of the correct options and none of the wrong ones.
  • Results by email: when you finish, your graded report (PDF attached, with the explanation of every option of every answered item) is emailed to the assessment team and to your own address.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: press A–E (or 1–5) to answer and Enter to continue. On the select-all-that-apply item, letter keys tick/untick options and Enter checks your answer.
