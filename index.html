How the Adaptive (CAT) Engine Works

  • One question at a time. Answer it, and you immediately see whether you were right or wrong — followed by an explanation of the correct option and of why every other option does not apply.
  • Every answer updates your ability estimate (a Rasch/3PL-style θ with a Bayesian posterior). The engine then selects the next question at the difficulty that measures you most precisely — easier items after a miss, harder items after a hit.
  • The exam can end early. Once the estimate is precise enough (standard error ≤ 0.36) and at least 25 items have been answered, the engine stops — exactly how the real CISSP CAT terminates. Maximum length is all 40 questions in the bank.
  • Scoring: raw score, percentage, and a CISSP-style scaled score of 100–1000 where 700 is the pass standard. Content is balanced across the eight CISSP domains.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: press A–D (or 1–4) to answer and Enter to continue.
